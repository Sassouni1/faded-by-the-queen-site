Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

FADED. by the Queen uses information you provide through our website, Facebook lead forms, and direct contact to respond to inquiries and provide grooming and hair consultation services.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, appointment details, and the information you include in a consultation request. Our website and service providers may also process basic technical information needed to operate the site and forms.

How we use it

  • Respond to consultation requests and arrange services.
  • Send service and appointment messages when you have requested them or consented to receive them.
  • Maintain our records, protect our services, and meet applicable obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers to host our website and manage forms, customer records, scheduling, and messages. These include Meta when you submit a Facebook form, HighLevel/LeadConnector for lead records, and GlossGenius when you choose to book an appointment. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share mobile information or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

Your choices

Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you provided, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. If we text you, you can reply STOP to stop messages. Promotional texts require a separate opt-in and are not required to request a consultation.
