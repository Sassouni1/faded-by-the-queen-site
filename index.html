MEET BRITTANY B

With over 10 years of experience in the beauty industry, I’m a licensed cosmetologist and licensed cosmetology instructor with a passion for precision, grooming, and creating an experience worth coming back to.

I created FADED. with a simple belief: men deserve more than just a haircut. They deserve the opportunity to slow down, relax, and enjoy quality grooming in an environment designed with them in mind.

For me, it’s about more than getting the cut right. From the details of the service to the atmosphere in the chair, I want every client to feel taken care of. Whether you’re here for a sharp fade, a clean beard, or a little time to reset, my goal is for you to leave looking your best, feeling relaxed, and—most importantly—happy with your experience.
