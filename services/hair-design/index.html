Hair Design at FADED. by the Queen

Hair Design is an add-on for a custom line, part, or design in a haircut. It can be a small finishing detail or a more visible element, depending on the design you discuss with Brittany.

What to expect from this service

Because it is listed as an addition to a haircut, choose the cut you want first and then describe the design. A reference image can help communicate placement and shape. The menu lists a starting price of $5.

FADED. by the Queen is Brittany B's appointment-only grooming studio at 16623 Birkdale Commons Pkwy #103A, Loft 4, Huntersville, NC 28078. Brittany is a licensed cosmetologist and cosmetology instructor with more than 10 years in the beauty industry.
