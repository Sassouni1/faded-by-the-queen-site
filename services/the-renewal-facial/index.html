The Renewal Facial at FADED. by the Queen

The Renewal Facial is the longer of Brittany's two general facial services. Its listed focus is hydration and revitalization in a 50-minute appointment.

What to expect from this service

If your goal is a simpler cleanse, exfoliation, and hydration service, the 45-minute Skin Refresh Facial is another option. The beard-specific facial focuses on the skin beneath facial hair instead.

FADED. by the Queen is Brittany B's appointment-only grooming studio at 16623 Birkdale Commons Pkwy #103A, Loft 4, Huntersville, NC 28078. Brittany is a licensed cosmetologist and cosmetology instructor with more than 10 years in the beauty industry. Her studio's approach is to make the appointment feel considered, from the service details to the time you spend in the chair.

How to choose the right appointment

These related services have a different focus:

  • Skin Refresh Facial: A cleansing, exfoliating and hydrating facial.
  • Beard Facial: Cleanse, exfoliate and condition the skin beneath the beard.

Compare what each includes before selecting a time, especially if you want to combine grooming details or choose between a shorter appointment and a fuller experience.
