The Maintenance at FADED. by the Queen

The Maintenance is a touch-up for the edges of a haircut. It focuses on the front hairline, temples, and neckline when your overall cut still works but the outline could use attention.

What to expect from this service

This is a practical option between full haircuts. It is different from an Express Cut, which is still a cut for clients arriving with clean, dry hair, and different from Beard Detail, which focuses on facial hair.

FADED. by the Queen is Brittany B's appointment-only grooming studio at 16623 Birkdale Commons Pkwy #103A, Loft 4, Huntersville, NC 28078. Brittany is a licensed cosmetologist and cosmetology instructor with more than 10 years in the beauty industry. Her studio's approach is to make the appointment feel considered, from the service details to the time you spend in the chair.

How to choose the right appointment

These related services have a different focus:

  • Express Cut: A cut for clients arriving with clean, dry hair; it does not include the signature grooming experience.
  • Beard Detail: Refine the beard, neckline, cheek line and mustache; also available with a haircut.

Compare what each includes before selecting a time, especially if you want to combine grooming details or choose between a shorter appointment and a fuller experience.

Planning your visit in Huntersville

Think about the finish you want and anything you would like to keep or change from your last appointment. A clear description or reference photo can help Brittany understand your preference.
