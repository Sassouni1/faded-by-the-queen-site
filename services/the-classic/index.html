The Classic at FADED. by the Queen

A classic cut is a versatile choice when you want your hair neat, intentional, and easy to wear. Brittany can work with an all-over shear or clipper approach, depending on the look you want and the length you have now.

What to expect from this service

This is the full FADED. experience: a precision haircut, shampoo, conditioning treatment, two hot towels, a neck and shoulder treatment, and finishing style. It is designed for someone who wants time in the chair as well as a finished cut.

FADED. by the Queen is Brittany B's appointment-only grooming studio at 16623 Birkdale Commons Pkwy #103A, Loft 4, Huntersville, NC 28078. Brittany is a licensed cosmetologist and cosmetology instructor with more than 10 years in the beauty industry. Her studio's approach is to make the appointment feel considered, from the service details to the time you spend in the chair.

How to choose the right appointment

These related services have a different focus:

  • The Fade: A tailored fade using multiple clipper lengths, plus the full grooming experience.
  • Express Cut: A cut for clients arriving with clean, dry hair; it does not include the signature grooming experience.

Compare what each includes before selecting a time, especially if you want to combine grooming details or choose between a shorter appointment and a fuller experience.

Planning your visit in Huntersville

Think about the finish you want and anything you would like to keep or change from your last appointment. A clear description or reference photo can help Brittany understand your preference. The posted menu price for The Classic is $40. Check the booking page for current availability. If you are unsure which option fits your goal, call before booking so you can choose the right service.

Frequently asked question

How is The Classic different from an express cut?

The Classic includes the full grooming experience. The express option is a cut for clients arriving with clean, dry hair and does not include those extras.
