Express Cut at FADED. by the Queen

An Express Cut is the shorter menu path when your hair is already clean and dry and you want to focus on the cut itself. There are express prices for a classic cut, fade, and bald fade.

What to expect from this service

This option does not include the signature FADED. experience. If you want shampoo, conditioning, hot towels, a neck and shoulder treatment, and finishing style as part of the appointment, choose one of the corresponding full haircut services instead.

FADED. by the Queen is Brittany B's appointment-only grooming studio at 16623 Birkdale Commons Pkwy #103A, Loft 4, Huntersville, NC 28078. Brittany is a licensed cosmetologist and cosmetology instructor with more than 10 years in the beauty industry. Her studio's approach is to make the appointment feel considered, from the service details to the time you spend in the chair.

How to choose the right appointment

These related services have a different focus:

  • The Classic: An all-over shear or clipper cut with the full FADED. grooming experience.
  • The Fade: A tailored fade using multiple clipper lengths, plus the full grooming experience.

Compare what each includes before selecting a time, especially if you want to combine grooming details or choose between a shorter appointment and a fuller experience.

Planning your visit in Huntersville

Think about the finish you want and anything you would like to keep or change from your last appointment. A clear description or reference photo can help Brittany understand your preference. The posted menu price for Express Cut is Classic $35 · Fade $40 · Bald Fade $45. Check the booking page for current availability.
