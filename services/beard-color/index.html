Beard Color at FADED. by the Queen

Beard Color is for clients who want to adjust the appearance of gray or refresh the tone of their facial hair. The menu describes subtle color or gray blending with a natural-looking finish.

What to expect from this service

Color choices are personal and depend on the beard you have now and the result you want. The site requires a consultation for this service, so discuss the desired shade and amount of blending before booking the color appointment.

FADED. by the Queen is Brittany B's appointment-only grooming studio at 16623 Birkdale Commons Pkwy #103A, Loft 4, Huntersville, NC 28078. Brittany is a licensed cosmetologist and cosmetology instructor with more than 10 years in the beauty industry. How to choose the right appointment

These related services have a different focus:

  • Beard Detail: Refine the beard, neckline, cheek line and mustache; also available with a haircut.
  • Gray Blending: Natural-looking gray blending; color consultation required.

Compare what each includes before selecting a time, especially if you want to combine grooming details or choose between a shorter appointment and a fuller experience.
